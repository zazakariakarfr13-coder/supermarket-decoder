A plain-language food-label glossary

A short reference for words you may see on packaged foods.

Ingredient list

The list of substances used to make the food. In many labelling systems, ingredients are presented in descending order by weight.

Nutrition information

A panel showing energy and nutrients such as fat, carbohydrates, sugars, protein and salt or sodium. Check the stated basis: per 100 g, per 100 ml or per serving.

Added sugar and sugar names

Sweetening ingredients may appear as glucose syrup, dextrose, invert sugar or fruit-juice concentrate. Maltodextrin is a starch-derived carbohydrate with different technological and nutritional properties from table sugar.

Additive / E-number

An identifier for a food additive in systems that use E-number labelling. The function and context matter more than the code alone.

Emulsifier

An ingredient that helps ingredients such as oil and water remain mixed and supports texture.

Stabiliser / thickener

An ingredient used to maintain or adjust a food's consistency and structure.

NOVA classification

A framework that groups foods by the nature and extent of processing. It is not a complete assessment of the nutritional quality of an individual food.

Label terminology and legal requirements vary across countries. Use the label on the product in your hand and consult local official guidance for regulatory questions.
