The 30-second food-label checklist

A quick shelf-side method: turn the pack over, read the first three ingredients, then hunt for signals.

1 · Turn it over

Look at the ingredient list and nutrition information, not only the marketing claims on the front.

2 · Read the first three

Ingredients are generally listed in descending order by weight. The first three give a quick snapshot of the recipe. Read the full list too.

3 · Hunt for signals

Notice ingredients such as glucose syrup, dextrose, maltodextrin, modified starches, flavourings and emulsifiers. Their presence helps explain how a product was formulated; do not assume every additive is automatically harmful.

Bonus · Compare the numbers

Use the per-100-g column to compare similar foods. Check sugars and salt, then consider other values in the context of the product and your needs.

Remember: Processing is not automatically the enemy. NOVA is a processing classification, not a complete health score. Consider the whole label and the overall pattern of your diet.
