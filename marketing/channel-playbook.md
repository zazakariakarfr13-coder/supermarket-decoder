# Channel playbook

## The funnel
Helpful free content → relevant discovery channel → Supermarket Decoder website → verified Gumroad checkout.

## Pinterest
- Publish original pins based on one educational point each.
- Use search-friendly titles and descriptions.
- Link to the matching free article or checklist.
- Review saves, outbound clicks and top-performing topics.

## Search engines
- Keep every page focused on one question.
- Use descriptive page titles and meta descriptions.
- Link related articles to one another and back to the homepage.
- Add the live sitemap to Google Search Console after deployment.
- Publish accurate explanations rather than keyword-stuffed pages.

## LinkedIn
- Lead with your food-industry learning and practical examples.
- Share a useful insight before mentioning the product.
- Invite questions and reply thoughtfully.
- Keep medical or health claims out of marketing copy.

## Reddit and forums
- Read community rules before posting.
- Answer the question without requiring a click.
- Disclose that you created the guide when mentioning it.
- Link only where self-promotion is permitted and genuinely relevant.

## Metrics to track
- Website sessions and unique visitors.
- Visits to free-resource pages.
- Outbound clicks to Gumroad.
- Pinterest saves and outbound clicks.
- Search queries and search impressions.
- Gumroad product-page visits and sales.

GitHub Pages is a static hosting service, not an advertising network. GitHub repository stars are not a substitute for qualified visitors or sales.
