# Pinterest plan — United States audience

## Funnel

**Useful Pin → matching free page → clear link to the $9 Supermarket Decoder guide.**

Do not send every educational Pin straight to checkout. Give the reader the answer promised by the Pin first; the free page can then introduce the complete guide.

## Board structure

Keep the existing **Food / healthy shopping** board, and create focused boards so each Pin has a clear home:

1. **Food Label Reading Tips** — serving sizes, ingredients, nutrition panels and label-reading routines.
2. **US Nutrition Facts Label Explained** — serving size, calories, Added Sugars, sodium and % Daily Value.
3. **Ingredients & Hidden Sugars** — syrups, dextrose, maltose, ingredient order and compound ingredients.
4. **Smart Grocery Shopping & Food Swaps** — practical product comparisons and simple supermarket swaps.

Use a clear board description with the main topic words. Save each Pin to the closest matching board rather than putting every Pin into the broad board.

## Publishing rules

- Keep the vertical 2:3 format used in the existing Canva batch (your current 666 × 1000 designs are 2:3).
- Use one clear promise per Pin, large mobile-readable text, a useful keyword in the title and a natural keyword-rich description.
- Use an accurate destination that fulfils the Pin's promise.
- Add clear alt text and a save prompt where appropriate.
- For the US audience, use US Nutrition Facts concepts: serving size, calories, Added Sugars, sodium, saturated fat and %DV. Do not use UK/EU per-100-g traffic-light thresholds as if they were US rules.
- Keep medical claims and fear-based language out of the creative.

## Weekly review

Review Pinterest Analytics once a week:

- **Impressions:** did Pinterest distribute the Pin?
- **Saves:** was it useful enough for people to keep?
- **Pin clicks:** did the creative earn a closer look?
- **Outbound clicks:** did it send people to your website?

Track Gumroad product-page visits and purchases separately. Change one variable at a time: topic, visual hook, destination or title. Avoid judging a Pin from one day's results.
