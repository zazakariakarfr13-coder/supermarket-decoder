## Publishing checklist
- Use one clear idea per pin and readable text at mobile size.
- Add descriptive alt text.
- Link to the most relevant free article.
- Avoid medical promises, fear-based claims or claims that every additive is harmful.
- Track saves and outbound clicks in Pinterest analytics.
