How the factory makes Instant ramen

wheat noodles are cut, steamed, pressed into blocks and flash-fried. Frying drives out the water and leaves tiny holes, so boiling water can rehydrate the noodles in about three minutes. Momofuku Ando invented the method in Japan in 1958.Alkaline: Made with mineral salts that make noodles springy, chewy and slightly yellow.

  2. The noodles are steamed

  4. The blocks are flash-fried until dry

The factory secret

🔒 What you can't easily copyThe frying isn't for flavour, it's a drying trick. The hot oil pushes the water out and leaves the noodle full of tiny holes. Hot water rushes back into those holes, which is why dinner takes three minutes.

How to make Instant ramen at home

  2. Steam the noodles, then twist them into small nests
  3. Fry the nests until dry and golden, then let them cool

Bake the baking soda on a lined tray at about 120°C for an hour, and wear gloves: it becomes strongly alkaline.

Questions people ask

Why does instant ramen cook so fast?

The noodles are steamed and then fried. Frying leaves tiny holes that let hot water back in quickly.

Is most of the salt in the ramen packet?

Yes, roughly three-quarters in most brands. The noodles themselves still contain some.
