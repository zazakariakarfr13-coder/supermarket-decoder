Can You Make Gummy bears at Home? 🐻

Yes. Gelatin, juice and sugar in a silicone mold make real gummy bears. The step almost everyone skips is drying them, and that's where the chew comes from.

How the factory makes Gummy bears

A stamps bear shapes into flat trays of cornstarch. Hot gelatin syrup is poured into the hollows, and the trays rest for a day or more. Then the bears are shaken out and given a thin coat of wax so they don't stick together.Starch mogul: A machine that stamps candy shapes into trays of cornstarch, then fills them.

  1. A starch mogul stamps bear shapes into cornstarch
  2. Hot gelatin syrup fills the shapes
  3. The bears dry for a day or more
  4. A thin wax coating stops them sticking

The factory secret

🔒 What you can't easily copyThe molds are made of starch, and the starch pulls moisture out of the bears while they set. That drying is what gives them their firm chew.

How to make Gummy bears at home

  1. Sprinkle gelatin over juice and let it soak for 5 minutes
  2. Warm it with sugar, a little corn syrup and until dissolvedCitric acid: A sour powder from citrus fruit. Mixed with baking soda and water, it fizzes.
  3. Fill a silicone mold, chill, then air-dry the bears for a day

Straight from the fridge they're soft and jelly-like. A day on the counter makes them chewy.

What the label tells you

Look for the wax at the end of the list, often carnauba wax or beeswax. That's the shine.

Questions people ask

How are gummy bears made in factories?

Hot gelatin syrup is poured into bear shapes stamped into trays of cornstarch, dried for a day or more, then lightly waxed.

Why are homemade gummies soft?

They haven't been dried. Leave them out for about a day to firm up.
