Can You Make Pop-Tarts at Home? 🍓

Yes. Pop-Tarts are pastry, jam and icing, and the homemade version is flakier and fruitier. Pie crust, jam and a simple glaze is all you need.

How the factory makes Pop-Tarts

Dough runs along a , filling is laid down in rows, a second sheet goes on top, and machines seal, cut, bake and frost the tarts.Sheeting line: Machines that roll dough into one long, perfectly even sheet.

  1. Dough is rolled flat on a sheeting line
  2. Filling is laid down in neat rows
  3. A top sheet goes on, then it's sealed and cut
  4. The tarts are baked, then frosted

The factory secret

🔒 What you can't easily copyThe first Pop-Tarts, launched in 1964, had no frosting at all. Frosted ones arrived in 1967, once Kellogg's had made a frosting that could survive the toaster.

How to make Pop-Tarts at home

  1. Roll out pie crust and cut it into rectangles
  2. Add a spoon of jam, put a second rectangle on top and crimp the edges with a fork
  3. Bake until golden, then glaze with powdered sugar and milk, and add sprinkles

Poke a few holes in the top so steam can escape, or the jam will burst out at the sides.

Questions people ask

When did Pop-Tarts get frosting?

In 1967, three years after launch. The first Pop-Tarts in 1964 were unfrosted.

What do you need to make Pop-Tarts at home?

Pie crust, jam, powdered sugar and milk for the glaze. Sprinkles are optional.
