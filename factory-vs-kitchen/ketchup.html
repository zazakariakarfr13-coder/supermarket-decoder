Can You Make Ketchup at Home? 🍅

Yes, in about fifteen minutes. A can of tomato paste, vinegar, sugar and a few spices gets you very close to the bottle.

How the factory makes Ketchup

Tomato is mixed with vinegar, sweetener, salt, onion powder and spices, cooked, smooth and bottled hot.Concentrate: The flavour base with most of the water left out. The water gets added later.Milled: Ground so finely that it becomes perfectly smooth.

  1. Tomato concentrate goes into a steam kettle
  2. Vinegar, sweetener, salt and spices are added
  3. It's cooked and milled smooth

The factory secret

🔒 What you can't easily copyThe "57" on Heinz bottles was marketing, not a count. Henry Heinz reportedly combined his lucky number 5 and his wife's lucky 7, at a time when the company already sold more than 60 products.

How to make Ketchup at home

  1. Whisk tomato paste with vinegar, a little sugar and salt
  2. Add onion powder and a pinch each of clove and allspice
  3. Simmer for 15 minutes, then cool and refrigerate

Keep it in the fridge and use it within a few weeks.

Questions people ask

What does 57 mean on Heinz ketchup?

It was a slogan. Henry Heinz reportedly picked 57 from his and his wife's lucky numbers; the company already had more than 60 products.

How do you make ketchup from tomato paste?

Whisk paste with vinegar, sugar, salt, onion powder and spices, then simmer for about 15 minutes.
