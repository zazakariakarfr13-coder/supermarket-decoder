How the factory makes Twinkies

Sponge cake batter is baked in canoe-shaped pans. Once cooled, the cakes are flipped and the cream filling is injected through three small holes in the bottom.

  1. Sponge cake batter is mixed
  2. It's baked in Canoe pan: A baking pan with long, narrow, rounded cups.
  3. The cakes are flipped over
  4. Cream is injected through three holes

The factory secret

🔒 What you can't easily copyFlip one over and you'll see the three injection holes. Shelf life is 45 days, set in 2013 (up from about 26), so the "lasts forever" story is a myth.

How to make Twinkies at home

  1. Bake a sponge batter in a canoe pan (or foil shaped into canoes)
  2. Whip butter with marshmallow cream for the filling
  3. Poke three holes underneath and pipe the filling in

Homemade ones are best within about three days.

Questions people ask

How long do Twinkies last?

45 days, since the 2013 relaunch. The "Twinkies last forever" idea is a myth.

How does the cream get inside a Twinkie?

It's injected from underneath through three small holes.
