How the factory makes Coca-Cola

Coca-Cola doesn't ship finished soda to every city. It makes a and sends it to bottlers, who add sweetener and water and fill the bottles and cans locally.Concentrate: The flavour base with most of the water left out. The water gets added later.Carbonated: Water with carbon dioxide gas dissolved in it. That's the fizz.

  1. The company makes the secret concentrate
  2. Concentrate is shipped to local bottlers
  3. Bottlers add sweetener and carbonated water

The factory secret

🔒 What you can't easily copyThe label just says "natural flavors". The written formula spent 86 years in a bank vault, and since 2011 it has been kept in a vault at the World of Coca-Cola museum in Atlanta.

How to make Coca-Cola at home

  1. Simmer sugar and water with orange, lemon and lime zest
  2. Add cinnamon, a little nutmeg, vanilla and a pinch of Citric acid: A sour powder from citrus fruit. Mixed with baking soda and water, it fizzes.
  3. Colour with caramel or burnt sugar, strain, and mix with sparkling water

Homemade cola is fresh and spicy, closer to a craft soda than to Coke.

What the label tells you

"Natural flavors" is where the secret hides. Companies don't have to say which flavourings are in that blend.

Questions people ask

Where is the Coca-Cola recipe kept?

In a vault at the World of Coca-Cola in Atlanta since 2011. Before that it was in a bank vault for 86 years.

Can you make Coca-Cola at home?

Not the real one. You can make a homemade cola with citrus, spices, vanilla and caramel.
