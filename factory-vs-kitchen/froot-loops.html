Can You Make Froot Loops at Home? 🥣

No. The light, puffy rings come from extrusion under high heat and pressure. Home-baked rings end up dense and crunchy instead, which makes them a different cereal.

How to make Froot Loops at home

  1. Make a sweet dough with flour, sugar and fruit zest
  2. Shape tiny rings by hand or with a piping bag
  3. Bake until crunchy, with food colouring if you like

They taste good, but they're closer to tiny cookies than to puffed cereal.
