Label-reading practice

Can you decode this ingredient list?

This is a fictional practice label created for learning. It is not a real brand, product or nutrition claim.

Fictional product: Cocoa Crunch Bites

Ingredients: whole-grain corn flour, sugar, cocoa powder, sunflower oil, malt extract, salt, natural flavour, soy lecithin.

Allergen note for this exercise: contains soy (from soy lecithin). A real package may have additional allergen statements and cross-contact information.

Try three questions

  1. Which ingredient is listed first?
  2. Which ingredients in this example can contribute sugars?
  3. What would you check before comparing this product with another?
Reveal the reasoning

1. Whole-grain corn flour. In many jurisdictions, ingredients are listed in descending order by weight, subject to local rules and exceptions. The first item is a useful clue, not a complete recipe analysis.

2. Sugar and malt extract. Both can contribute sugars. Ingredient names alone do not tell you the total amount per serving; use the nutrition panel and serving size.

3. Compare like with like. Check serving size, sugars and other relevant nutrients, the complete ingredient list, and allergen information. The best choice depends on context, price and personal needs.
