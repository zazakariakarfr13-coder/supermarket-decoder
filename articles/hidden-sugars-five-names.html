Ingredient-list decoding

Five names to recognise when scanning food labels

Sugar does not always appear as the single word “sugar”. Include other sweetening ingredients and starch-derived carbohydrates in your scan.

1 · Glucose syrup

A sweet syrup made from starch. It can contribute sweetness, moisture and texture.

2 · Dextrose

Another name for glucose. It is used as a sweetening ingredient and can also have technological roles in a recipe.

3 · Invert sugar

A mixture of glucose and fructose produced by breaking down sucrose. It helps sweeten foods and can influence texture and moisture.

4 · Fruit-juice concentrate

A concentrated fruit juice ingredient. It can contribute sugars as well as fruit flavour; the name alone does not tell you how much is present.

5 · Maltodextrin

A starch-derived carbohydrate used for functions such as bulking, carrying flavours or affecting texture. It is not simply another name for table sugar.

What to do with this information

Read the full ingredient list and check “of which sugars” in the nutrition panel where it is provided. Do not judge a product from one word alone: quantity, recipe, product type and your own needs matter.
