Five clues for the drink aisle

01 · FRONT

“Electrolytes”, “energy” and “real fruit” are prompts to check, not facts about your day.

02 · COLUMNS

On bigger bottles, read the per-container column if you'll drink it all.

03 · JUICE %

Drinks that claim juice must say how much. 5% is a cameo, not a starring role.

04 · SUGARS

Total Sugars includes fruit's own sugar; Added Sugars shows what was added. About 4 g ≈ 1 teaspoon.

05 · CAFFEINE

Check for caffeine or sources such as coffee or guarana; the amount is often voluntary on U.S. labels.

Final checkpoint

Three questions. No moose.

1. A 1-litre bottle shows two columns. You'll drink the whole thing. Which column?

2. A carton says “Made with real fruit” and “contains 5% juice”. How much of the drink is juice?

3. Must a U.S. Nutrition Facts label say how many milligrams of caffeine a drink contains?
