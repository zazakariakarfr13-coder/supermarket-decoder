The method

The 30-second scan

Three steps, always in the same order. After a week of practice, you'll do it without thinking.

2 SEC
1

Turn it over

Ignore the front. The back is information; go straight to the ingredient list.

10 SEC
2

Read the first three

Ingredients are listed by weight. The first three are most of what you're buying.

15 SEC
3

Hunt for signals

One ingredient you'd never use at home is enough to mark a product as ultra-processed.

Why trust this guide?

ZF

Written by Zakaria Froud, a food-industry technician in training. The guide explains not only what's on the label, but why factories add it: shelf life, texture, cost. Every fact is sourced from the WHO, EFSA, EU law and peer-reviewed studies, with a full source list in Chapter 10.

Questions

Good to know

What format is it?

A 53-page PDF (A4), designed to read on a phone, tablet or computer. The cheat-sheet and the 14-day tracker are printable.

Will it tell me what to eat?

No. It teaches you to read labels and compare products, then suggests simple swaps. You decide. It is for education only and is not medical or nutritional advice.

Does it work outside Europe?

Yes. Chapter 8 compares US, EU/UK and Moroccan/Middle Eastern labels, and shows how to convert sodium to salt, per serving to per 100 g, and more.

Do I need to buy expensive organic food?

No. Every swap in the guide is marked as cheaper, similar or a little more than what you buy now. Most are cheaper.
